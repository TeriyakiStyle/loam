Eroded rock is not yet fertile soil. You must invite life into it by providing food and water. And the best way to send that invitation is composting.

Think of compost as a carefully prepared banquet. By preparing a moistened pile of carbon-rich browns and nitrogen-rich greens, you're setting the table for billions of microscopic guests.

With a little work—turning, watering, and waiting—your pile transforms as countless organisms break down the material into finished compost.

The result is dark, crumbly organic matter with an earthy smell; your compost is ready!

Add this to your loamy soil to set the table for a wonderful exchange!
